#+BEGIN_QUOTE
From the shell of a snail's back to the galaxy we call home, if you follow a spiral inward, where does it actually end?
#+END_QUOTE

The Snake who once bit its tail had grown restless in youth. Equal steps had given it a
ring ([[id:frag-roundness][Roundness]]). Now it wanted to know how else it could move 

So it grew its step out.

#+BEGIN_SRC paperlang
fw 50
rt 90
fw 60 # length increases +10 each time
rt 90
fw 70
rt 90
fw 80
rt 90
fw 90
# ...
#+END_SRC

50. Then 60. Then 70. The turn remained the same right angle, but each length
outgrew the last, and the tip that had once waited under the chin slipped further away with every
coil. The Snake was calling itself coiling outwards without knowing how to stop.


#+BEGIN_SRC paperlang
def coil len do
  fw len
  rt 90
  coil len+10
end

coil 50

#+END_SRC

An Owl watched perched above the maze.

"Your coil will not close," said the Owl.

"I closed before."

"You kept the same step then. You are not keeping it now."

"Then where do I end?"

"When you stop calling," said the Owl. "You do not know yet, only I do."


The trail is a spiral. The snakes rule gives rise to the spiral. Make a similar turn, go forward, call
yourself again. When the length of the step stayed the same, the mouth bit its own tail. When the steps change,
the mouth never finds the tail, and the turning that never ends leaves behind this open maze instead.

And then, when does the Snake stop, it should be an unending dance.
Something in the Dojo already keeps a ceiling on self-calling. The End was real.
It was not yet the Snake's decision.

"You stopped me," said the Snake.

"The Dojo stops what will not stop itself," said the Owl. "That is mercy, not knowing. Knowing is
when the End lives in your own song."

"Teach me."

"Decide when to stop. When it is not, do not call yourself."

#+BEGIN_SRC paperlang
def coil x do
  fw x
  rt 90
  wait 0.01
  when x<100 do
    coil x+10
  end
end

coil 0
#+END_SRC

=when x<100= is the Owl's gift, carried in the climb: go on only while the tread is still below a
hundred. The moment the condition turns false, the next call is not made, and there is silence.
The trail is still a spiral. The song now ends where the Snake chose.

Forever and until are the same climb. One of them asked whether to go on.

- Move the End. Try =when x<20=, then =when x<200=. Where does the trail still look finished, and
  where does the room's ceiling take over if you climb past three hundred and sixty?
- Two ways to finish. A =loop 12= counts its end and stops. A =when= asks and stops when the answer
  turns false. When would you reach for each? ([[id:frag-the-endless-chain][The Endless Chain]]
  counts; this night asks.)
- Soften the well. =rt 92= instead of =rt 90=. The leftover angle is the old closed walk still
  hiding. For the mouth and the tip again, return to [[id:frag-roundness][Roundness]].
- Climb down. =coil 100= with =coil x-1=. Inward, toward the shell. What happens at nought?
  ([[id:frag-shapeshifting][Shapeshifting]].)
- Delete the =when=. Does the quiet fall in the same place as before?
- Four dancers, each chasing the next, share this hunger in a ring:
  [[id:frag-the-whirlpool][The Dancers' Whirlpool]].

When the second song ended, the Snake looked up.

"Can I go on forever? ", it asked.
