** Correction retained from the first pass

The first run collected transitions only while =tick= ran, so its reply, drain and
hand-input zero counts measured windows where collection was *off*. They could not show
that no handler ran. The re-run opens a phase-tagged window across each controlled wake; a
zero inside the window is the falsifier, a zero outside it is nothing.
The zero is certified, not assumed. A positive control forces a breath through the same
window machinery: a sync and an async window each record it, while an idle async window
records nothing. EFFECTS is reachable only from =stepFrame= via =stepOnce=, i.e. only
inside a tick; a wake window with no tick is structurally empty. A wake zero therefore
says "no pump step", not "no handler ran" — the eligibility fact still lives in frame
state.

** What an existing transition does not report

- Credit debt: the frame holds =owed= / suspension =credit=, yet the parked tick's only
  routed transitions are =breath/continue=. The debt is raised by the deposit fallback,
  which is not a routed verb. Residency shares that fallback and was not exercised:
  recorded, not bounded.
- Every controlled wake opens with *zero* routed transitions, collection live:
  admission reply =0=, admission fault =0=, credit return =0=, derived-move input =0=.
- Wake is not a suspension clear. The window facts separate them:
  - admission readiness arrives with the =admission= suspension *retained* (verdict set,
    same run); it is not a clear.
  - credit availability arrives with the =owed= debt *retained*; the replay and clear are
    later and internal to the next pass, and leave no routed transition.
  - a derived move queues with *no* suspension at all.
  - an admission *fault* clears the suspension as the run dies (suspension to null,
    =done= to true). A blanket clear notification would join a wake to a death.
- Drained is not committed. The queued derived move is consumed and, in this fixture, the
  pose commits ([0,0,0] to [7,0,0]); the two are reported as separate facts. The
  =redrawDerivedPlace= guards (=scheduler.js:2227-2232=) consume the entry and keep the
  last pose when the target is not done, the observer is not live, the law owner changed,
  or =applyLaw= does not commit. A failed redraw is not exercised here.
- The =tick= boundary collapses breath, debt and pending admission into one =building=
  boolean.

** Verdict

The two observed seams expose no complete stop/eligibility account: stops are partly
visible, wakes are not, and a "wake" would be three unlike things (an admission verdict, a
returned credit, a queued place) plus a termination clear. *This does not rule out an
O(transitions) account* — only this pair of seams. Promotion rejected; no runtime source
was changed. The leap from "these seams do not report readiness" to "each owner must gain a
reporting mechanism" is architectural, not earned: existing state may suffice, a scan may be
inexpensive, and a small pump-boundary summary may suffice. The cost of those alternatives is
unmeasured (see [[id:live-rendering-machinery-results]]). Do not mint reporting obligations
before measuring.
