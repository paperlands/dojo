* Predictions, before the cross
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: probe-fresh-attempt-plan
:LENS: empiricist adversarial
:END:

=node probe/state-model/fresh-attempt.mjs=. Scratch only, built-in =faceto=
with one already-bound being in an installation. Not quoted lookup, computed
point expressions, effectful argument replay, or a new meaning for figures.
Use the real resolver, chart conversion, motion and law gate. Once acquired,
the being identity is not acquired again on retry. Reject or complete one
observed command; at most one replay, no new run, no new queue.

- Standing restart is a positive reference. Live receiver/peer control should
  reproduce wrong old output, not simplify by discarding every reply.
- Stamp only: peer change retries correctly, receiver change still proposes
  from the old cursor. Adding a sampled receiver local pose and basis should
  agree with an independent world-space aim-and-step calculation, even when
  both participants move and the receiver's chart is rotated and translated.
- A whole-configuration stamp may reject a valid attempt when only an unrelated
  being moves. Compare with the used inputs (retained subject position, receiver
  local pose and basis): an unrelated edit does not make those values stale.
  Changed inputs still reject, with the original law and source/run gate intact.
- After two incompatible edits, exhaust the budget without moving or running
  the following command. Replacing the source while pending must never let the
  old result write. Completed pure figures still redraw, and a same-question
  waiting installation is not restarted or reread before its authored wait.

* Readings — separate the sampled geometry from the guard
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: probe-fresh-attempt-results
:LENS: empiricist adversarial
:MODULE: assets/js/turtling/scheduler.js, assets/js/turtling/executor.js
:END:

Six arms, same authored inputs, real parser and deferred responder. Only
scheduler and executor in disposable mirrors are changed. The live-policy
fixture limits spatial changes to installation bodies; private figures keep
the standing read semantics. The JSONL output includes both sampled inputs
and actual verdicts; assertions use an independent aim-and-step calculation
in world coordinates rather than an endpoint from the baseline.

A distinct control changes the receiver’s /basis/ by stated placement
instead of moving its local cursor. The stamp-only arm happened to pass
that case: resampling the basis sees a basis change, even with the old
local cursor. Testing both doors is essential. Both participants edited in
a translated, 90-degree rotated room: the coherent arm reaches
(19.316718,-9.341641,4), starting from the newly accepted (22,-8,4)
toward (12,-13,4), within 1e-8 of the independent oracle.

A global configuration stamp can reject even the no-edit case (revision
9→10 between initial offer and reply; observed poses unchanged). Two edits
to the same unrelated spare while a global-stamped attempt and its retry
were parked exhaust the budget and stop the command; comparing the used
inputs completes without retry. Two edits to actual inputs exhaust the
budget in both arms: no accepted aim, no following forward command, and
the hand's position remains intact. The footprint arm uses the global
revision as a cheap unchanged check, not as the last word on relevance.
It compares exact subject identity/position and receiver local pose/basis.

* The no-op is a handoff too
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: probe-fresh-attempt-noop
:LENS: adversarial architect
:END:

Move fox and prey together to (10,0,0) while the first aim is pending. The
stale reply is correctly rejected. The retry sees coincidence and produces
no proposal. Without a cursor handoff, the following =fw 3= starts at
(0,0,0) and erases the hand, ending at (3,0,0). The last arm copies the
sampled local cursor on this synchronous no-op; it issues no aim verdict
and the forward starts at 10, ending at 13. A no-op isn't permission to
publish a new pose; it is a completed read from which the local execution
cursor may continue.

All arms preserve source replacement: no verdict from the retired writer
lands on the namesake destination. The distance law still refuses a forward
step at radius 5, and the completed pure figure still redraws. In the last
arm a peer move during a wait triggers zero aim reads or offers before the
authored deadline; then the original run reads once and moves correctly.

* Disposition
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: probe-fresh-attempt-disposition
:LENS: architect
:END:

An attempt can be inspectable data: exact target identity, observed point,
receiver local pose and storage basis, and a revision hint. A fresh attempt
does not authorize a retired run; a fresh run does not make an old attempt
current. Keep the original acceptance and law checks. No production change:
this is bounded to one already-bound =faceto= subject, no arbitrary argument
replay. Before promoting, fence coincident continuation and independent
retirement, challenge the footprint against pre-instant/cycle observations,
and do not apply this rule to quoted lookups, effectful arguments, or every
coordinate consumer merely because it has a pose.
